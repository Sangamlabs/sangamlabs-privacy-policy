Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sangamlabs (“we”, “us”, or “our”) may collect, use, store, and protect information when you contact us or use our digital services, including WhatsApp-based customer support and sales automation.

Please read this policy before sharing personal or project information with Sangamlabs. Do not send passwords, one-time passcodes (OTPs), payment card details, private keys, or other highly sensitive credentials through chat.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on your inquiry and the services you request, we may process:

  • Contact details, such as your name, WhatsApp phone number, and messages you send us.
  • Project information, requirements, reference links, preferred features, budget range, and requested deadlines.
  • Customer-support and order records, including inquiry status, quotation status, and communications relevant to your project.
  • Technical information that a service provider makes available when you interact with our website, forms, or digital services.

Please share only information needed to discuss or deliver your project.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to respond to questions, understand project requirements, prepare quotation requests, manage customer support, maintain order records, improve our services, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not treat an inquiry as an accepted order until the required review and approval steps are completed.

3. WhatsApp and AI-Assisted Responses

If you contact us through WhatsApp, your messages and contact details may be processed through Meta’s WhatsApp services and our configured automation tools. We may use AI services to help understand messages and draft relevant responses. AI-generated responses can be inaccurate, so quotations, commitments, and order approvals may require review by an authorized Sangamlabs representative.

WhatsApp and other third-party services process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Their availability and data handling are not controlled entirely by Sangamlabs.

4. Storage and Service Providers

Depending on the workflow used for your inquiry, information may be stored in business tools such as spreadsheets or databases and processed by automation, hosting, messaging, or AI providers. These tools are used to support customer communications and project administration. Access should be limited to people and systems that need the information for these purposes.

Examples of providers that may be involved in a configured workflow include Meta/WhatsApp, n8n, Google services, MongoDB-related hosting, and AI model providers. The exact providers depend on the service and workflow used for your interaction.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information with service providers that help us operate our services, when you ask us to coordinate with a third party, when required by law, or when necessary to protect our rights, users, or systems. We aim to share only what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

6. Retention and Security

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to handle your inquiry, provide services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods can vary by record type and the settings of the systems used. We use reasonable safeguards intended to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping needs. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Some information may remain in backups or records that must be retained for legal, security, or operational reasons.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for people who can legally request the relevant services in their location. Please do not provide a child’s personal information unless you are legally authorized to do so.

9. External Links and Third-Party Services

Our messages or services may include links to external websites or platforms. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We encourage you to review those policies before sharing information with them.
